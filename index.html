• Any soldering, board modifications, or physical tampering

How this works

Miner Carfax tracks runtime, temperature, fan duty, and overclock level to estimate remaining life on replaceable parts. Estimates are based on typical Bitaxe wear patterns and are not lab measurements. Use them to plan simple swaps (fan, paste, PSU) or decide when to contact the seller for warranty items. Always check your specific seller’s warranty terms before modifying.
